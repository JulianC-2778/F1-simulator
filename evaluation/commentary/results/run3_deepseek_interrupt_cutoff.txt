Cutoff for evaluation/commentary/results/real_experiment_latency_raw_20260808_run3_deepseek.jsonl

(time.monotonic() on this machine, recorded right after switching

everything before the cutoff when building the final latency CSV; do not
try to salvage or backfill it.
